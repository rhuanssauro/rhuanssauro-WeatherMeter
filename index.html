    About the data

    What these numbers are

    Past days from the Forecast API are an archived forecast (past_days). Days from today forward are a forecast. When the Historical Weather API answers, those past days are labeled reanalysis. Reanalysis is a model reconstruction, not a sensor at the pin.

    Swell height and total wave height come from the Marine API and stay in separate columns. The marine horizon is often shorter than 15 days. Empty future days stay empty.

    The conditions column is an application classification for satellite-link weather, onshore and offshore. It is not fade in dB and it does not implement ITU-R P.618. Thresholds: daily rain ≥ 5 mm or an hourly rain ≥ 2 mm flags rain on the path; hourly rain ≥ 10 mm flags heavy rain; mean cloud ≥ 80% or a low, mid, or high cloud hour ≥ 90% flags extensive cloud; gusts ≥ 30 kt flag gusts; WMO codes 95, 96, and 99 flag thunderstorm. Offshore only, swell ≥ 2.0 m or total wave ≥ 2.5 m flags sea state that can move a floating or unstabilized antenna.

    Use this page as a weather and sea-state view. Link margin, antenna pointing, and navigation decisions stay with the operator and the proper tools.
